Zhaohui Lin | 林昭辉

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, supervised by Dr. Huixu Dong. My research focuses on developing precise, and highly generalizable manipulation policies for dexterous robotic hands.

Previously, I was a research intern at Shanghai AI Lab, where I specialized in reinforcement learning and imitation learning for dexterous manipulation tasks.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Zhejiang University, where I actively participated in robotics competitions and gained hands-on experience in embedded systems, motion control, and computer vision. Drawing on these experiences, I conducted my undergraduate thesis on industrial robotic visual seal coating, which has been accepted for publication in the IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics.

    Research

    My research interest is broadly in Robotics and Perception, with particular interests in dexterous manipulation, reinforcement learning, and imitation learning currently. My research objective is to build a dexterous robotic agent with the robust and generalizable ability to perceive and interact with a complex real-world environment.

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, supervised by Dr. Huixu Dong. My research focuses on developing smoother, more precise, and highly generalizable manipulation policies for dexterous robotic hands.

Previously, I interned with Dr. Jiangmiao Pang’s group at Shanghai AI Lab, where I specialized in reinforcement learning and imitation learning for dexterous manipulation tasks.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Zhejiang University, where I actively participated in robotics competitions and gained hands-on experience in embedded systems, motion control, and computer vision. Drawing on these experiences, I conducted my undergraduate thesis on industrial robotic visual seal coating, which has been accepted for publication in the IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics.
